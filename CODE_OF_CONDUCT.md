## 1. Purpose & Community Values

SAMANVAYA · SŪTRA (legacy: `AgentBridge`) is built on principles of **local data sovereignty, zero-trust security, transparency, and collaborative engineering**. 

We cultivate an open-source development culture where software engineers, security researchers, system operators, and automated agent developers collaborate with mutual respect, intellectual rigor, and professional integrity.

Our core community values:
1. **Safety & Integrity First:** We prioritize security, privacy, and system safety in all discussions, code reviews, and decisions.
2. **Constructive Collaboration:** We focus on evidence-based technical merit, constructive problem-solving, and shared learning.
3. **Empathy & Inclusivity:** We welcome individuals of all backgrounds, identities, skill levels, and perspectives.
4. **Accountability:** We take responsibility for our words, technical decisions, and impact on collaborators.

---

## 2. Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## 3. Expected Standards of Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, architectures, and engineering experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall security, reliability, and health of the project community.
- Using inclusive, welcoming, and professional language in pull requests, issues, discussions, and commit messages.

---

## 4. Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information, such as physical addresses, phone numbers, or private credentials, without their explicit permission (doxxing).
- Dismissive, patronizing, or hostile behavior toward newcomers, reviewers, or contributors.
- Malicious distribution of exploits, backdoors, or uncoordinated security vulnerability details outside designated security channels.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## 5. Scope of Application

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces associated with the project, including:

- Repository issues, pull requests, commit discussions, and code reviews.
- Project GitHub Discussions and release forums.
- Official chat channels, issue trackers, or documentation review rooms.
- In-person or virtual events representing SAMANVAYA · SŪTRA.

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces, such as presenting at a conference, speaking on a podcast, or engaging on social media with official project affiliations.

## 6. Reporting Process

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported to project maintainers.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Maintainer Contact Configuration Notice:**  
> The project maintainers have not yet designated a public, dedicated email address for conduct reports.  
> **Current Reporting Route:** `[MAINTAINER_DECISION: Configure public reporting contact email or GitHub Security Advisory form here]`  
> **Interim Procedure:** Until an official dedicated reporting mailbox is published, please submit reports privately via **GitHub Repository Private Vulnerability Reporting / Security Advisories** or reach out directly to the repository maintainers through their private GitHub profile contacts. Do not post conduct grievances in public issue threads.

When filing a report, please include:
1. Date, time, and location (e.g., link to PR, issue comment, or chat log) of the incident.
2. The identities of the parties involved.
3. A factual description of the behavior and context.
4. Any relevant screenshots, log excerpts, or supporting materials.
5. Details on whether this is an ongoing situation.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and investigated impartially.

## 7. Privacy & Confidentiality

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident:

- The identity of the reporter will remain confidential unless disclosure is strictly required by legal process or explicitly authorized by the reporter.
- Investigation details will be discussed only among designated maintainers who do not have a conflict of interest in the matter.
- Any maintainer who has a personal or direct conflict of interest with a report must recuse themselves from deliberations.

## 8. Maintainer Responsibilities & Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## 9. Enforcement Guidelines & Consequences

Maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action deemed in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction
- **Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.
- **Consequence:** A private, written warning from maintainers, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning
- **Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions demonstrating disrespect or boundary violations.
- **Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban
- **Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment, or deliberate disruption of project work.
- **Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time (e.g., 30 days). No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban
- **Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained harassment, publication of private credentials or personal data, threats of violence, or intentional sabotage of project integrity.
- **Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project community, including commit access, issue creation, and pull request participation.

---

## 10. Appeals & Reconsideration Process

Individuals subjected to an enforcement action may appeal the decision:

1. Submit a written appeal to the maintainer group within 14 calendar days of receiving notice of the enforcement action.
2. The appeal must outline reasons why the decision should be reconsidered, including any new factual context or evidence.
3. The maintainers (excluding any recused parties) will review the appeal and deliver a written final resolution within 14 business days. The determination of the maintainers upon review is final.
